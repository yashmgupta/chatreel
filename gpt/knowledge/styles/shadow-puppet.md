# shadow-puppet

Adapted from Lemomo/LemoLab, Lemo-Opuscar styles/shadow-puppet/STYLE.md. CC BY 4.0; see ATTRIBUTION.md.

Reference only: unavailable in this renderer. Never submit this style to /render. Real-footage styles are excluded from this project.

Materials & rendering:
1. **Cloth**: procedural weave (sin × sin, noise-warped), fibre speckle, low-frequency density blotches, darker edges at the frame. **Post**: warm-tinted bloom, a **warm vignette** (toward brown, never grey), slight saturation, soft S-curve, light grain; heat…

Type & subtitles:
1. **Titles are puppets**: dark hide plaques with letters cut out (the implementation + the implementation, so words glow), a carved border, carried on rods, pressed on to enter, lifted off to

Motion quality:
1. **24 fps continuous** for puppets and camera; only swapped effect pieces (fire, water) step at 12
2. **Hand tremor** on every puppet (a few px, ~0.02 rad, low-frequency), stronger under

Sound palette:
1. **Percussion first, not piano and strings** (gong-and-drum pattern thinking, 锣鼓经): low barrel drum, hard high clapper drum (woodblock), cymbals, small gong with rising pitch ("tai"), big gong with falling pitch ("kuang"); also a bamboo clapper board, pellet…
2. **Bowed and reed voices**: a bright nasal fiddle (erhu-like: pitched up, nasal formant, pitch-curve resampling for slides and vibrato), plain pentatonic (**huan-yin**) or sorrowful microtonal (**ku-yin**: raised 4th, lowered 7th); a shrill suona-like reed…

Native moves:
1. **The light is the world.** Lamp count drives heat and colour. *Fits content like:* city lights spreading at night; a fever rising and falling; servers switching on one by
2. **Off the screen = big and blurry.** A piece pulled toward the lamp grows and blurs. *Fits content like:* a rumour growing; a memory fading; a deadline pressing onto the cloth and snapping
