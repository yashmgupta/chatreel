Materials & rendering:
1. **Paper**: warm off-white (around the implementation). The tooth is **anchored to the page** (it pans with the camera; on zoom two octaves cross-fade so grains stay ~1 px on screen), never screen-fixed: three octaves of value noise plus a horizontal fibre…
2. **Wax adhesion (the core rule)**: every crayon layer is a canvas where RGB = colour and **A = pressure**; the shader deposits wax where the implementation (soft edge). Light pressure = speckled peaks; heavy = almost solid, slightly

Colour logic:
1. **A box of about a dozen crayons, never more.** Outlines in one dark colour (indigo, dark brown, plum), **never black**. One white crayon reserved for wax
2. **At most one watercolour** per film, chosen for what it reveals (ultramarine for night, rose for dawn, sap green for rain). It never paints the

Motion quality:
1. **Everything drawn steps at 12 fps** (lines, colouring, characters, boil seed); camera, the wash's leading edge and twinkles run at 24
2. **Boil**: each stroke re-jitters offset (<1 px), wobble and hatching rows every 2 frames. Characters full, background lines ~0.6, background colouring still (it also halves the bitrate). Calm the boil to about half when the film goes
