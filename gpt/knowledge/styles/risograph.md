Essence, and what it is not:
1. **A tiny palette**: 2–3 spot inks on warm off-white
2. **Overprint mixing**: inks multiply, so two inks make a third colour and all three approach black; a tint of one ink over a solid of another gives the brightest

Colour logic:
1. **2–3 spot inks, never more**, plus paper; every other colour is an overprint or a tint. No gradients except stepped

Type & subtitles:
1. **Type is printed**: titles and subtitles live on one plate, so they misregister and grain with it. A title can be printed on two plates with a visible offset: the riso
