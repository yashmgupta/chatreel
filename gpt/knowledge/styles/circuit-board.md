Materials & rendering:
1. 1. **Laminate (FR-4):** dark green, a glass-weave basket texture in two crossfaded octaves so its on-screen size stays bounded at every zoom. Bottom-layer copper may show through
2. 2. **Copper pour:** fills the board; everything is a **clearance** punched out of it (channels, lakes, sea, keep-outs around parts and traces). Rendered twice: a lighter rim pass with the plain clearance, then the fill pass with the clearance grown by ~1.6…

Pitfalls of the medium:
1. **Flat pour.** A board that is all mid-green reads as an empty slide. Fill with real board texture: stitching vias that stay visible at map scale, fences along every clearance, mottling, micro silk, other unlit routes that exist in the
2. **Decorative routing.** Traces that mean nothing make viewers ask what they are. Draw only real routes; texture comes from vias and mask, not invented
