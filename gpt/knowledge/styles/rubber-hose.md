Materials & rendering:
1. **Frame**: 1920×1080 output with a **4:3 gate** (1440×1080 centred, black pillarbox), gate corners rounded (r ≈ 28 px), soft inner shadow on the gate
2. **Characters**: flat fills, one hard-edged form-shadow band (light grey) on the side away from an upper-left light, ink outline **constant in screen space** at any zoom (transform points first, then stroke; ~6 px at 1080p), light **line boil** (±1.2 px, 3…

Colour logic:
1. **Strictly greyscale**: about 6 grey steps plus a separate character white. No hue anywhere, not even in the film
2. **Characters own the extremes**: character whites and ink blacks are brighter and darker than anything in the background. Backgrounds live in the middle greys (walls around mid grey, furniture one or two steps darker). Add a mild contrast boost (≈ 1.1–1.15)…

Type & subtitles:
1. **Subtitles are a period title card**: a small near-black plate with a white double-rule border and dot-and-ring corner ornaments; text in **IM Fell English SC** (~36 px), paper white. Drawn in the scene layer so it weaves and flickers with the film; pops on…
2. **Titles**: **Shrikhand** fat retro script, white face, heavy ink outline (~14 % of the cap height), ink drop shadow down-right; letters can hop in a travelling wave on the beat. Sub-lines in **Limelight**

Sound palette:
1. **1930s hot dance band / ragtime**: stride piano, clarinet, trumpet (open + plunger-mute "wah-wah"), trombone (smears), tuba oom-pah, banjo on 2 & 4, snare with brushes and rolls, xylophone, woodblocks, slide whistle, cymbal, kazoo, washboard, a pit organ.…
2. **Mickey-mouse the score, as options**: a band member "answering" a character (trombone raspberry, trumpet laugh); a stop (hard silence, reverb tails too) before a gag or on a stare; a stinger per gag; slide whistle for every stretch or fall; a prop that *is*…
