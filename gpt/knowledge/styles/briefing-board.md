Materials & rendering:
1. **Board (z 0)**: procedural enamel, wiped ghost strokes, smudges, a window falloff, a sliding diagonal reflection; the frame shadows a warm grey
2. **Ink (multiply)**: flat colour at about 90 %, lighter core, round caps, a wet-tip glint on the stroke being drawn. Lettering: a marker font revealed glyph by glyph with seeded tilt and baseline jitter. Erasing leaves a blurred ghost (about 7

Type & subtitles:
1. **Marker lettering**: a single-weight marker face covering every script the film uses (ours: LXGW Marker Gothic, OFL, subset — re-run when words change). Content 100–160 board px; a verdict
2. **Printed labels** (tags, cards, stamps): a sturdy sans; credit lines in a monospace. A tag holds one to three

Native moves:
1. **The whole-wall reveal.** Design the wall as a poster first (hierarchy, reading path, filling the frame), then plan every shot as a crop of it; keep one zone (secondary layer, verdict, title card) for the reveal alone. *Fits:* a product roadmap; a household…
2. **The subject passes through the answer.** The real thing moves through the chosen solution, its sound first. *Fits:* a parcel along the chosen route; a train over the new

Pitfalls of the medium:
1. **Text glimpsed in passing** fails the reading time. Fix: words where the camera rests; frame each new item with the last
2. **Only close-ups.** The viewer never sees the whole object against the limit. Fix: detail → judgement frame → hold → verdict → move
