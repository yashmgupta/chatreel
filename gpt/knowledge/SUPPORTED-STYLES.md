# Supported style ports

Adapted from Lemomo/LemoLab Lemo-Opuscar. CC BY 4.0; see ATTRIBUTION.md. Only the five IDs below are valid. Use the compact implemented grammar; broader reference prompts describe capabilities not implemented here.

## swiss-motion
Use paper, black ink and one warm signal color. Compose on a 12-column grid. Use exact eased wipes and straight camera moves. Never add gradients, bounce or grain. Keep type flush left; spare space gives shapes mass. Original harmonic pulse music.

## blueprint
Use one blue engineering sheet with pale technical lines. Reveal outlines then dimensions and figure labels. Use orthographic 2D shapes; do not promise 3D orbit, glow or a realistic machine. Keep notes in the lower reserved lane. Mechanical partials accompany drafting.

## chalkboard
Use a clean deep-green board with wooden frame and restrained chalk grain. Draw simple lesson geometry progressively; white for structure, one yellow accent. Do not promise calligraphic stroke order or authentic Chinese handwriting. Keep text and objects apart; soft decaying tones.

## woodcut
Use a black relief plate on cream paper. Light consists of carved outlines and hatching; reserve one accent for the focus. Captions sit in the paper margin. Do not use smooth gray shading or claim a full form-following original shader. Inharmonic struck sound.

## pixel-rpg
Render at 320x180 then scale nearest-neighbor. Step actor movement at 8 Hz; use simple geometric sprites and a dialog box. Keep all action above the dialog. No bloom, CRT or 3D. Pulse-wave score. Do not promise original upstream rigs, battle systems or palette-era transitions.
