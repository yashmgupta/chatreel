# A place on the grid — sample treatment

Three structures considered: a countdown, an inventory, and a small signal seeking company. Chosen: a three-step journey because it gives a simple geometric actor a visible change.

| Time | Shot/action | Narration | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Push toward a lone warm circle facing a dark block | One signal, looking for a place. | Sparse pulse |
| 5–10 | Pan across the gap, circle drifts | The gap asks for a different move. | Added cue on second scene |
| 10–15 | Locked composition, shapes nearer, circle pulses | A new rhythm brings us together. | High cue at resolution |
